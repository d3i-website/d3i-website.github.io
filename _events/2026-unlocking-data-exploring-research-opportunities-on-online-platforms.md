  Academic research plays a critical role in shaping a safer online ecosystem. The Digital Services Act (DSA) creates opportunities for researchers and NGOs to study online platforms in greater depth. In addition to publicly available information, eligible researchers can gain access to non-public platform data under specific provisions of the DSA.

  During this webinar, the Netherlands Authority for Consumers and Markets (ACM) will explore these topics together with relevant experts.

  Save the date for ACM’s webinar on November 20 to discover how these new opportunities can advance your own research.
