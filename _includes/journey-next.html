{%- comment -%} "Next step" wayfinding for pages that are a step in a hub-journey section (Software, Prepare a study). The hub page's `movements` list is the single source of truth: this include finds the hub for the current page's sidebar section, locates the step whose link matches the current URL, and renders the step after it (number, title, body, link), plus a quieter "After that" line when a further step exists. Renders nothing on the last step, on pages that are not steps, and in sections without a journey hub. {% include journey-next.html %} URLs are compared with trailing slashes stripped, because the two sections differ in whether their permalinks carry one. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign journey_nav = page.sidebar.nav -%} {%- if journey_nav -%} {%- assign journey_hub = site.pages | where: "hub_section", journey_nav | first -%} {%- if journey_hub and journey_hub.movements and journey_hub.movements.size > 0 -%} {%- assign journey_here = page.url | split: "/" | join: "/" -%} {%- assign journey_next_i = -1 -%} {%- for journey_step in journey_hub.movements -%} {%- if journey_step.link and journey_step.link.url -%} {%- assign journey_step_url = journey_step.link.url | split: "/" | join: "/" -%} {%- if journey_step_url == journey_here -%} {%- assign journey_next_i = forloop.index -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- if journey_next_i >= 0 and journey_next_i < journey_hub.movements.size -%} {%- assign journey_next = journey_hub.movements[journey_next_i] -%} {%- assign journey_after_i = journey_next_i | plus: 1 -%} {%- assign journey_after = nil -%} {%- if journey_after_i < journey_hub.movements.size -%} {%- assign journey_after = journey_hub.movements[journey_after_i] -%} {%- endif -%}
