Initially starting out as a PhD network, we broadened our focus to include all early career researchers who are interested in exchange and networking around the topic of data donations. To do so, we send emails to our mailing list occasionally and offer a Slack channel for networking. We also plan workshops with input on the topic of data donations and organize meetups around conferences of interest to the data donation community.

**Interested? Join our mailing list** [**here**](https://mailings.uu.nl/hp/BsTM1OxHcH5nTdpEqXAkMg/subscribe_newsletter_data_donation_network)

Questions? Just reach out to us, the current Early-Career Representatives: [Lion Wedel](<mailto: lion.wedel@weizenbaum-institut.de>) and [Elisabeth Schmidbauer](<mailto: Elisabeth.schmidbauer@ifkw.lmu.de>).
