The frame every content slide shares: an eyebrow naming the part over a `slide-title` at the top margin, the body in the column below, and one pinned footer row with the site name left and the slide number right. Its commonest body is a row of three cards hanging from one line, each a picture and a few words.

**Words.** About 20, counted as the brand book's Slides · Voice says. The preview has 20: a card's eyebrow names its platform, since a mark never stands alone. Each card's `body` adds words, and so does `panel`; they are off in the preview, and the 20 words still apply when you turn them on. A frame with only a panel or children, no cards, is a text-only slide: give it 50 words at the very most, and prefer a picture that carries the point.

Mount `D3I.SlideFrame` in a design canvas; for a Slides deck, `D3I.slideMarkup(D3I.SlideFrame, props)` returns the slide as one `<section>` with inline hex styles to paste. Its props are in `components/index.d.ts` and on its card; the values below are what it draws, for anyone writing a slide by hand.

Pass `eyebrow` ("Part II · The method"), a `heading` that states a finding or names a topic in one line (two at most), and `number`, or `source` for a citation in the footer's left slot. `cards` takes up to three siblings (`title`, optional `iconSrc`/`iconAlt` and `eyebrow`, which names a platform mark's platform, and the word-adding `body`); `panel` takes the statement columns (optional `eyebrow`, `text`); anything else goes in as children between them.

- Section: `surface`, padding 128px 128px 136px, a flex column with 48px gaps, base line height 1.4. The body (cards, children, panel, 48px apart) is centred in the space between the title block and the bottom margin, so a slide of few words is not top-heavy. The title block is `slide-eyebrow` in `text-muted` (Nunito Sans 800, caps), 16px above `slide-title` in `heading`, both `data-fit="none"`. The title is the serif (Libre Caslon Condensed, provisional) at 420, 64px, line height 1.08, no tracking.
- Cards on a line: the cards have no frame, fill or corner. The row has 40px of padding on top, and one 3px `blue` line runs across it 12px from its top edge: it starts at a ring node 48px into the left margin (x 80 on the slide), a filled `blue` node (20px) sits over the first 64px of each card, where its icon is, and it ends at a ring node on the right margin (x 1792). Ring nodes are 16px `surface` discs in a 3px `blue` ring. Each card is a column with 12px between lines and 32px clear on its right: the icon (12px more below it), the eyebrow in `text-muted` if given, the title in the serif at 420, 48px, line height 1.18, in `heading`, and one line of `slide-body` if given. A 64px icon may head a card: a platform mark from `assets/Platforms` when the card is about a platform, or a concept glyph from `assets/Glyphs` when it is about a kind of trace with no platform.
- Statement panel: `surface`, no corner, a 2px `border` hairline round it and between its columns; each column has padding 32px 40px, a `text-muted` eyebrow over `slide-body`, 12px apart.
- Footer: pinned 64px from the bottom between the 128px margins; two `slide-label` lines in `text-muted`, or the source in `slide-small`.
- Budget: the flow is 816px tall (1080 minus 128 and 136) and ends 41px above the footer. A title block is about 115px, the row of cards on its line about 200 (about 280 with eyebrows and a body line), a statement panel about 160.
