Two forms that let a slide's layout make its point rather than a row of matching cards: a funnel, for a sequence of shrinking shares, and a meter, for options that differ in how much of something reaches someone. Use one when the things on a slide differ in kind or size; use plain cards only when they are parallel.

Mount `D3I.SlideFunnel` in a design canvas; for a Slides deck, `D3I.slideMarkup(D3I.SlideFunnel, props)` returns the slide as one `<section>` with inline hex styles to paste. Its props are in `components/index.d.ts` and on its card; the values below are what it draws, for anyone writing a slide by hand.

Pass `funnel` (`eyebrow`, `base`, three or four `stages` of `label` and `pct` against that base, a `note`, and `markLast: false` when the last stage is not the point) and/or `meter` (two to four `options`, each `eyebrow`, `fill`, `fillLabel`, `text`, and `recommended` on the one the project advises), with `heading`, `eyebrow` and `number`.

- The title block: the eyebrow in `text-muted` caps, the title in the serif (Libre Caslon Condensed, provisional) at 420, 64px.
- Funnel: a `surface` card framed by a 2px `border` hairline, square, padding 32px, 16px between blocks; its eyebrow in `text-muted` caps. Bars are 26px tall and square, in `blue`, widths proportional with the first at 400px. The last bar is `coral`, the one spot of colour that marks the point (the share that completed); pass `markLast: false` to keep every bar `blue`. The percentage sits at the end of its bar in Nunito Sans 800 at 24px, never in a column at the right edge, so number and bar read together. (`color` from the earlier funnel is ignored.)
- Meter: one card per option, `surface`, square, padding 24px, framed by a 2px `border` hairline; the recommended card's frame is 2px `blue` and its eyebrow `blue`, the others' eyebrows `text-muted`. In each card a 180px box framed by a 2px `border` hairline, filled from the bottom: `all` is solid `blue` with `on-accent` text (everything arrives); `promise` is a 3px dashed `blue` outline with `blue` text (arrives, then deleted); `strip` is a 28px `blue` strip with `on-accent` text (only the selected part).
- Other layout-as-argument forms: one framed box with three tall bands and a single small `coral` square for a proportion; a statement in a framed panel in `slide-heading` for a finding; a line with a node per step for a sequence (SlideBuild).
- Corners are square and nothing is tinted or shadowed.
