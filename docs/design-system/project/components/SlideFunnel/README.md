Two forms that let a slide's layout make its point rather than a row of matching cards: a funnel, for a sequence of shrinking shares, and a meter, for options that differ in how much of something reaches someone. Use one when the things on a slide differ in kind or size; use plain cards only when they are parallel.

Mount `D3I.SlideFunnel` in a design canvas; for a Slides deck, `D3I.slideMarkup(D3I.SlideFunnel, props)` returns the slide as one `<section>` with inline hex styles to paste. Its props are in `components/index.d.ts` and on its card; the values below are what it draws, for anyone writing a slide by hand.

Pass `funnel` (`eyebrow`, `base`, three or four `stages` of `label` and `pct` against that base, a `note`, and `color: "blue"` for a second funnel on a slide) and/or `meter` (two to four `options`, each `eyebrow`, `fill`, `fillLabel`, `text`, and `recommended` on the one the project advises), with `heading`, `eyebrow` and `number`.

- Funnel: a `coral-tint` card (`blue-tint` for a blue funnel), padding 32px, 16px between blocks. Bars are 26px tall, `radius-md`, widths proportional with the first at 400px; the percentage sits at the end of its bar in `slide-label` at 800, never in a column at the right edge, so number and bar read together.
- Meter: one 180px box per option on `surface-strong`, filled from the bottom: `all` is solid `coral` with `heading` text (everything arrives); `promise` is `coral-tint` with a 3px dashed `coral` border and `coral` text (arrives, then deleted); `strip` is a 28px `blue` strip with `on-accent` text (only the selected part). The recommended card takes `blue-tint` with a `road` border and a `blue` eyebrow; the others are `surface` with a `border` and `text-muted` eyebrows.
- Other layout-as-argument forms: one bordered box with three tall bands and a single small `coral` square for a proportion; a statement in a tint panel in `slide-heading` for a finding; a rail with stops for a sequence (SlideBuild).
